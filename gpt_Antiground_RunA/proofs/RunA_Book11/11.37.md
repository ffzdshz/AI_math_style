## Proposition 11.37
**Statement:** If four straight lines be proportional, the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described will also be proportional; and, if the parallelepipedal solids on them which are similar and similarly described be proportional, the straight lines will themselves also be proportional.

**Step 1.** Let a, b, c, d be the four straight lines, and let K, L, M, N respectively be the similar and similarly described parallelepipedal solids on them, with these lines as corresponding edges. We use 11.33 directly, since it gives precisely the relation between these solids and their corresponding edges. For the converse, we will construct a fourth proportional rather than assume that equality of triplicate ratios can be cancelled. [11.33]

**Step 2.** First suppose that a:b = c:d. By successive applications of 6.11, construct straight lines p, q, r, s such that
 a:b = b:p = p:q,
 c:d = d:r = r:s.
[6.11]

**Step 3.** The hypothesis and these constructions give equal corresponding successive ratios in the sequences a, b, p, q and c, d, r, s. Therefore, ex aequali,
 a:q = c:s.
[5.11, 5.22]

**Step 4.** By the definition of triplicate ratio and 11.33,
 K:L = a:q and M:N = c:s.
Consequently K:L = M:N. This proves the forward implication. [D5.10, 11.33, 5.11]

**Step 5.** Conversely, suppose that K:L = M:N. Construct a fourth proportional e to a, b, c, so that
 a:b = c:e.
On e describe a parallelepipedal solid T of the same prescribed similar form and corresponding situation as the given solids. [6.12, 11.27]

**Step 6.** The forward implication established in Steps 2–4 gives K:L = M:T. Together with the present hypothesis, this yields M:N = M:T. Magnitudes to which the same magnitude has the same ratio are equal; hence N = T. [5.11, 5.9]

**Step 7.** Construct straight lines u and v such that
 d:e = e:u = u:v.
Since N and T are similar solids on the corresponding edges d and e, their ratio is the triplicate ratio of d:e; thus
 N:T = d:v.
But N = T. Taking single copies of the four magnitudes in the definition of equal ratios therefore gives d = v. [6.11, D5.10, 11.33, D5.5]

**Step 8.** If d were greater than e, the proportion d:e = e:u would imply e > u, and the proportion e:u = u:v would then imply u > v. Thus d > e > u > v, contrary to d = v. If d were less than e, the same applications would give d < e < u < v, again contrary to d = v. Therefore d = e. [5.14]

**Step 9.** Since d = e, we have c:e = c:d. Combining this with a:b = c:e gives a:b = c:d. Thus the proportionality of the solids implies the proportionality of the straight lines, and both assertions are proved. [5.7, 5.11]
