## Proposition 11.12
**Statement:** To set up a straight line at right angles to a given plane from a given point in it.

**Step 1.** Let Π be the given plane and A the given point in it. Choose a point B outside Π. From B draw BC perpendicular to Π, meeting Π at C. [11.11]

**Step 2.** If A lies on BC, then BC itself is the required straight line through A, since it is perpendicular to Π. [11.11] Otherwise, join AB. [P1] The intersecting straight lines AB and BC lie in one plane Σ, which therefore contains A and BC. [11.2]

**Step 3.** In Σ, draw through A a straight line AD parallel to BC. [1.31] The chosen route is to transfer the known perpendicularity by this parallel construction, rather than establish individual right angles afresh.

**Step 4.** Since BC is perpendicular to Π and AD is parallel to BC, AD is also perpendicular to Π. [11.8] Thus AD is a straight line set up at right angles to the given plane from the given point A in it, as required.
