## Proposition 11.10
**Statement:** If two straight lines meeting one another be parallel to two straight lines meeting one another not in the same plane, they will contain equal angles.

**Step 1.** Let the first pair of straight lines meet at A and the second pair at D, and denote them by AB, AC and DE, DF respectively, where AB ∥ DE and AC ∥ DF. Each intersecting pair lies in a plane by 11.2; by hypothesis, the four lines do not all lie in one plane.

**Step 2.** Join AD (P1). By 11.7, AD lies both in the plane of the parallels AB, DE and in the plane of the parallels AC, DF. Choose B and E on the same side of AD in the first of these planes, and C and F on the same side of AD in the second. Thus the chosen rays represent corresponding directions. Shortening the longer of each pair of segments when necessary, arrange that AB = DE and AC = DF (1.3).

**Step 3.** Join BE, CF, BC, and EF (P1). By 11.7, BE lies in the plane of AB and DE, and CF lies in the plane of AC and DF. Apply 1.33 to the equal parallel segments AB and DE, whose corresponding endpoints are A, D and B, E: it gives AD ∥ BE and AD = BE. Applying 1.33 likewise to AC and DF gives AD ∥ CF and AD = CF. This is the direct path: 1.33 supplies both the parallelism and the equality of the joining segments at once.

**Step 4.** The three lines AD, BE, and CF cannot all lie in one plane. Otherwise, by 11.7, their joining lines AB, DE, AC, and DF would all lie in that plane, contrary to the hypothesis. Since BE and CF are parallel to AD and are not all coplanar with it, BE ∥ CF by 11.9. Moreover, BE = CF, since each equals AD (CN1).

**Step 5.** The endpoint correspondences established in Step 3 are A with D, B with E, and C with F. Hence BC and EF join the equal parallel segments BE and CF at corresponding ends. Applying 1.33 again gives BC = EF.

**Step 6.** The triangles ABC and DEF are each planar by 11.2. Their corresponding sides satisfy AB = DE and AC = DF by Step 2, and BC = EF by Step 5. Therefore their angles contained by the first two pairs of equal sides are equal by 1.8: ∠BAC = ∠EDF. Thus the two pairs of intersecting straight lines contain equal corresponding angles, as required.
