## Proposition 1.15
**Statement:** If two straight lines cut one another, they make the vertical angles equal to one another.

**Step 1.** Let the straight lines AB and CD cut one another at O, with A, O, B in that order and C, O, D in that order. We must prove ∠AOC = ∠BOD and ∠COB = ∠DOA. [Given configuration.]

**Step 2.** Let T denote the sum of two right angles. Every pair of right angles has this same sum: right angles are equal, and adding equals to equals gives equal wholes. [P4, CN2.]

**Step 3.** Use 1.13 directly, since straightness is given and we need the adjacent-angle sums; no auxiliary construction or converse straightness test is needed. Applying it to OC standing on AB, to OB standing on CD, and to OA standing on CD, respectively, gives
∠AOC + ∠COB = T,
∠COB + ∠BOD = T,
∠DOA + ∠AOC = T.
[1.13; Step 2.]

**Step 4.** The first two sums in Step 3 are equal because both equal T. [CN1.] Subtract the common angle ∠COB from these equal sums. The remainders are equal, so ∠AOC = ∠BOD. [CN3.]

**Step 5.** The first and third sums in Step 3 are likewise equal because both equal T. [CN1.] Subtract the common angle ∠AOC. The remainders are equal, so ∠COB = ∠DOA. [CN3.] Thus both pairs of vertical angles are equal, as required.
