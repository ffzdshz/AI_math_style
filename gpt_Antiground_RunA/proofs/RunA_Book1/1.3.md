## Proposition 1.3
**Statement:** Given two unequal straight lines, to cut off from the greater a straight line equal to the less.

**Step 1.** Let AB and CD be the given unequal straight lines, with AB > CD. At A place a straight line AE equal to CD. [1.2] Path choice: use the previously proved length-transfer construction directly, rather than reconstructing it from elementary constructions.

**Step 2.** Describe a circle with centre A and distance AE, and let F be the point where its circumference meets AB in the direction from A toward B. Since AE = CD < AB, this meeting occurs between A and B. [P3; Step 1 and the given inequality]

**Step 3.** Since E and F lie on the circumference of the circle with centre A, AF = AE. [D1.15]

**Step 4.** But AE = CD, so AF = CD. [CN1] Thus AF, lying within AB, has been cut off from the greater straight line AB equal to the lesser straight line CD, as required.
