## Proposition 1.42
**Statement:** To construct, in a given rectilineal angle, a parallelogram equal to a given triangle.

**Step 1.** Let ABC be the given triangle and θ the given rectilineal angle. Bisect BC at D, so that BD = DC, and join AD. (1.10, P1.)

**Step 2.** Through A draw a straight line ℓ parallel to BC, producing straight lines as necessary throughout the construction. (1.31, P2.) The triangles ABD and ADC stand on the equal bases BD and DC and lie between the same parallels BC and ℓ; therefore they are equal in area. (1.38.) Path choice: the equal-base theorem applies directly to these distinct half-bases; we will construct a parallelogram double of triangle ADC.

**Step 3.** At D construct a ray r on the same side of BC as A, making with DC an angle equal to θ. (1.23.) Let r meet ℓ at E. To justify this intersection, if the straight line containing r did not meet ℓ, it would be parallel to ℓ. (D1.23.) It would consequently be parallel to BC, since BC is also parallel to ℓ. (1.30.) This is impossible, since these distinct lines meet at D. (D1.23.) Thus the intersection exists; it lies on the chosen ray because ℓ lies on A's side of BC. Hence ∠CDE = θ by construction. (1.23.)

**Step 4.** Through C draw a straight line m parallel to DE. (1.31.) It meets ℓ at a point F: otherwise m would be parallel to ℓ, and DE and ℓ, being parallel to m, would be parallel to one another, contrary to their meeting at E. (D1.23, 1.30.) Now DC is parallel to EF, and DE is parallel to CF, by the two parallel constructions. Thus DCFE is a parallelogram, and its angle CDE equals θ. (1.31, Step 3.)

**Step 5.** The parallelogram DCFE and triangle ADC have the same base DC and lie between the same parallels BC and ℓ. Therefore the parallelogram is double of triangle ADC. (1.41.)

**Step 6.** By Step 2, triangle ABD equals triangle ADC. Adding triangle ADC to each, and observing that AD divides triangle ABC into triangles ABD and ADC, triangle ABC equals two copies of triangle ADC. (CN2.) The parallelogram DCFE equals those same two copies by Step 5, so it equals triangle ABC. (CN1.) Therefore DCFE is the required parallelogram, equal to the given triangle and having an angle equal to the given rectilineal angle.
