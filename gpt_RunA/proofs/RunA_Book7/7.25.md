## Proposition 7.25
**Statement:** If two numbers be prime to one another, the product of one of them into itself will be prime to the remaining one.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be prime to one another; thus the unit alone is their common measure. [Hypothesis; D7.12]

**Step 2.** Let C be the number produced by multiplying A by itself. [D7.15]

**Step 3.** Choose the direct application of 7.24, which does not require its two factors to be distinct. Take both factors to be A and the number to which they are prime to be B. Each factor is prime to B by the hypothesis, so their product C is prime to B. Thus the product of A into itself is prime to the remaining number, as required. [7.24]
