## Proposition 7.17
**Statement:** If a number by multiplying two numbers make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will have the same ratio as the numbers multiplied.

**Step 1.** Let the number M, by multiplying the numbers A and B, produce P and Q respectively. By the definition of multiplication, P consists of as many copies of A as there are units in M, and Q consists of that same number of copies of B. [D7.15]

**Step 2.** Therefore A measures P the same number of times that B measures Q; thus P is the same multiple of A that Q is of B. [D7.5] By the definition of proportional numbers, P:A = Q:B. [D7.20]

**Step 3.** The direct path is numerical alternation, since Step 2 already supplies a proportion of four numbers. Applying alternation gives P:Q = A:B. [7.13] Hence the numbers produced have the same ratio as the numbers multiplied, as required.
