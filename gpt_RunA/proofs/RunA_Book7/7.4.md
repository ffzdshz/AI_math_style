## Proposition 7.4
**Statement:** Any number is either a part or parts of any number, the less of the greater.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be arbitrary numbers, with A less than B. Choose the direct definitional route: distinguish whether A measures B, since this is precisely the distinction between “a part” and “parts” in D7.3 and D7.4.

**Step 2.** If A measures B, then A is a part of B, because the less number is a part of the greater when it measures the greater. [D7.3]

**Step 3.** If A does not measure B, then A is parts of B, because the less number is called parts of the greater when it does not measure the greater. [D7.4]

**Step 4.** Either A measures B or it does not, so these cases are exhaustive. Therefore any number is either a part or parts of any greater number, as required. [D7.3, D7.4]
