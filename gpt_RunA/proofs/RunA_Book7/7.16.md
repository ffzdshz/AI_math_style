## Proposition 7.16
**Statement:** If two numbers by multiplying one another make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will be equal to one another.

**Step 1.** Let A multiplying B produce C, and let B multiplying A produce D. Let u be a unit. By the definition of multiplication, B measures C as many times as u measures A. [D7.15]

**Step 2.** Apply 7.15 to u, A, B, C: the unit u measures B as many times as A measures C. This is the direct route, since 7.15 applies exactly to the measuring relation obtained in Step 1, without introducing proportions. [7.15]

**Step 3.** Since B multiplying A produces D, A measures D as many times as u measures B. Thus, by Step 2, A measures C and D the same number of times. [D7.15]

**Step 4.** Consequently, C and D are each composed of the same number of copies of A. Adding these equal copies term by term gives equal wholes, so C = D. Therefore the numbers produced by the two numbers multiplying one another are equal. [CN2]
