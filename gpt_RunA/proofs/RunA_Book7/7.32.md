## Proposition 7.32
**Statement:** Any number either is prime or is measured by some prime number.

**Step 1.** Let A be any number. If A is measured by the unit alone, then A is prime, so the first alternative holds. [D7.11]

**Step 2.** Otherwise, A is measured by some number and is therefore composite. [D7.11, D7.13]

**Step 3.** Apply 7.31 directly, since Step 2 supplies precisely its composite-number hypothesis: A is measured by some prime number. Together with Step 1, this proves that any number either is prime or is measured by some prime number. [7.31; Steps 1–2]
