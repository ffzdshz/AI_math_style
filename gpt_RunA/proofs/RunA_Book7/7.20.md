## Proposition 7.20
**Statement:** The least numbers of those which have the same ratio with them measure those which have the same ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less.

**Step 1.** Let a and b be the least numbers having their ratio, and let A and B be any numbers such that a:b = A:B. By leastness, a ≤ A and b ≤ B. We use alternation because the hypothesis is already a proportion; this directly compares each least number with its corresponding number. [Hypothesis; D7.20.]

**Step 2.** By alternation, a:A = b:B. If a = A, this proportion gives b = B, and each least number measures its corresponding number once. It therefore remains to consider a < A. [7.13; D7.20.]

**Step 3.** Since a < A, a is either a part or parts of A. Suppose that a does not measure A. Then a is parts of A, and, because a:A = b:B, b is the same parts of B. [7.4; D7.3; D7.4; D7.20.]

**Step 4.** Unpacking “the same parts,” there are equal component parts e of A and corresponding equal component parts f of B such that A and B contain the same number of their respective component parts, while a and b each comprise the same number r of their respective component parts. Here r is greater than one: otherwise a would itself be one of the equal parts measuring A, contrary to the supposition. Consequently, e measures a exactly as many times as f measures b. [D7.3; D7.4; D7.20.]

**Step 5.** Hence e:a = f:b, and alternation gives e:f = a:b. But e < a and f < b, since each is one component of a whole containing more than one such component. Thus e and f are smaller corresponding terms having the ratio of a and b, contrary to their leastness. The supposition in Step 3 is therefore impossible, so a measures A. [D7.20; 7.13; CN5; leastness hypothesis.]

**Step 6.** Since a measures A, a is a part of A. From a:A = b:B, b is the same part of B, so b measures B exactly as many times as a measures A. Therefore the least numbers measure every pair having their ratio the same number of times, the greater the greater and the less the less. [D7.3; D7.20.]
