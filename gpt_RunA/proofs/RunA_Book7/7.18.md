## Proposition 7.18
**Statement:** If two numbers by multiplying any number make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will have the same ratio as the multipliers.

**Step 1.** Let the numbers A and B, by multiplying the same number C, produce D and E respectively. Thus D is obtained by taking C as many times as there are units in A, and E by taking C as many times as there are units in B. We must prove D:E = A:B. [D7.15]

**Step 2.** By commutativity of multiplication, C multiplying A also produces D, and C multiplying B also produces E. [7.16] This reversal is the direct path: it gives a common multiplier, precisely the hypothesis of 7.17.

**Step 3.** Since C, by multiplying A and B, produces D and E respectively, the products have the same ratio as the numbers multiplied: D:E = A:B. [7.17] Therefore the numbers originally produced have the same ratio as the original multipliers, as required.
