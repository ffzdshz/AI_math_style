## Proposition 5.5
**Statement:** If a magnitude be the same multiple of a magnitude that a part subtracted is of a part subtracted, the remainder will also be the same multiple of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be the whole magnitudes, and let C and D be the parts subtracted from A and B respectively. Suppose that A is the same multiple of B that C is of D. Denote the respective remainders by R and S. (Given hypothesis.)

**Step 2.** Choose the direct application of 5.1, whose statement as supplied has exactly these hypotheses. Applied to the wholes A, B and the subtracted parts C, D, it establishes that R is the same multiple of S that A is of B. Thus the remainder is the same multiple of the remainder that the whole is of the whole, as required. (5.1.)
