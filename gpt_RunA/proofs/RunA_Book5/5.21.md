## Proposition 5.21
**Statement:** If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, then, if ex aequali the first magnitude is greater than the third, the fourth will also be greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and if less, less.

**Step 1.** Let the three magnitudes be A, B, C, and the other three D, E, F, with the perturbed proportions
A:B = E:F and B:C = D:E (D5.18).
We must show that D is greater than, equal to, or less than F according as A is greater than, equal to, or less than C.

**Step 2.** From A:B = E:F obtain B:A = F:E by D5.5. Indeed, for arbitrary positive integers m and n, the comparison of nA with mB agrees with that of nE with mF. Reversing both comparisons, the comparison of mB with nA agrees with that of mF with nE, which establishes the inverse proportion by D5.5. This inversion is the direct path: the first triple now supplies ratios with common antecedent B, and the second supplies ratios with common consequent E.

**Step 3.** Suppose A>C. Then B:C > B:A, since the same magnitude has a greater ratio to the less than to the greater (5.8). By D5.7, there are positive integers m and n such that mB>nC while mB≤nA. Since B:C = D:E and B:A = F:E, D5.5 gives mD>nE and mF≤nE. These comparisons establish D:E > F:E by D5.7. Therefore D>F, since of magnitudes having ratios to the same magnitude, that which has the greater ratio is greater (5.10).

**Step 4.** Suppose A=C. The same magnitude B has the same ratio to the equal magnitudes A and C, so B:A = B:C (5.7). Combining this with B:A = F:E and B:C = D:E gives D:E = F:E (5.11). Magnitudes having the same ratio to the same magnitude are equal; hence D=F (5.9).

**Step 5.** Suppose A<C. Then B:A > B:C (5.8). By D5.7, there are positive integers m and n such that mB>nA while mB≤nC. The proportions B:A = F:E and B:C = D:E consequently give mF>nE and mD≤nE (D5.5). Thus F:E > D:E (D5.7), and therefore F>D (5.10), or D<F.

**Step 6.** By Steps 3–5, in the perturbed proportion the fourth magnitude D is greater than, equal to, or less than the sixth F according as the first A is greater than, equal to, or less than the third C, as required.
