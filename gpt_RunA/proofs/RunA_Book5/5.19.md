## Proposition 5.19
**Statement:** If, as a whole is to a whole, so is a part subtracted to a part subtracted, the remainder will also be to the remainder as whole to whole.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be the wholes, C and D their respective subtracted parts, and R and S the respective remainders. Thus A = C + R and B = D + S, and, by hypothesis, A:B = C:D.

**Step 2.** By alternation, A:C = B:D (5.16). The direct path is to place each whole over its own subtracted part, so that separation yields the remainders.

**Step 3.** Since A = C + R and B = D + S, the preceding proportion is (C + R):C = (D + S):D. Therefore, by separation, R:C = S:D (5.17).

**Step 4.** Alternating this proportion gives R:S = C:D (5.16).

**Step 5.** By the hypothesis, A:B = C:D, and by Step 4, R:S = C:D. Ratios which are the same with the same ratio are also the same with one another; hence R:S = A:B (5.11). Therefore the remainder is to the remainder as the whole is to the whole.
