## Proposition 6.24
**Statement:** In any parallelogram the parallelograms about the diameter are similar both to the whole and to one another.

**Step 1.** Let ABCD be a parallelogram with diameter AC, and let O lie between A and C. Through O draw EG parallel to AD, meeting AB at E and CD at G; also draw HF parallel to AB, meeting AD at H and BC at F [1.31]. Then EG is also parallel to BC, and HF to CD [1.30]. Thus AEOH and OFCG are the two parallelograms about the diameter.

**Step 2.** The angle at A of AEOH coincides with the angle at A of ABCD, and the angle at C of OFCG coincides with the angle at C of ABCD [CN4]. The angles AEO and OFC each equal ABC, by the parallel lines [1.29]. Since opposite angles of a parallelogram are equal, the remaining corresponding angles are equal as well [1.34, CN1]. Consequently both smaller parallelograms are equiangular with ABCD, with respective vertex correspondences
A,E,O,H ↔ A,B,C,D,
and O,F,C,G ↔ A,B,C,D.

**Step 3.** Triangles AEO and ABC are equiangular: their angles at A coincide, while their other corresponding angles are equal by EO ∥ BC [CN4, 1.29]. Path choice: apply 6.4 directly, since equiangularity is already established. Taking the sides about the equal angles E and B in both orders gives
AE:EO = AB:BC,
and EO:AE = BC:AB [6.4].

**Step 4.** Opposite sides of the two parallelograms are equal:
AE = OH, EO = HA, AB = CD, and BC = DA [1.34]. Substituting these equal sides into the proportions of Step 3 gives
AE:EO = AB:BC,
EO:OH = BC:CD,
OH:HA = CD:DA,
HA:AE = DA:AB.
Thus the sides about every pair of corresponding equal angles are proportional. Together with Step 2, this proves that AEOH is similar to ABCD [D6.1].

**Step 5.** Triangles OFC and ABC are likewise equiangular: their angles at C coincide, and their other corresponding angles are equal because OF ∥ AB [CN4, 1.29]. Applying 6.4 to the sides about the equal angles F and B, in both orders, gives
OF:FC = AB:BC,
and FC:OF = BC:AB [6.4].

**Step 6.** Opposite sides give OF = CG and FC = GO, while AB = CD and BC = DA [1.34]. Hence the proportions of Step 5 yield
OF:FC = AB:BC,
FC:CG = BC:CD,
CG:GO = CD:DA,
GO:OF = DA:AB.
Combined with the corresponding angle equalities of Step 2, these proportions prove that OFCG is similar to ABCD [D6.1].

**Step 7.** Both AEOH and OFCG are similar to the same rectilineal figure ABCD; therefore they are similar to one another [6.21]. Thus the parallelograms about the diameter are similar both to the whole and to one another.
