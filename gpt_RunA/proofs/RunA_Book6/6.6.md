## Proposition 6.6
**Statement:** If two triangles have one angle equal to one angle and the sides about the equal angles proportional, the triangles will be equiangular and will have those angles equal which the corresponding sides subtend.

**Step 1.** Let ABC and DEF be triangles such that ∠BAC = ∠EDF and AB:AC = DE:DF, with AB corresponding to DE and AC to DF. These are the given hypotheses.

**Step 2.** At E construct a ray on the same side of DE as F, making with ED an angle equal to ∠ABC [1.23]. The sum ∠BAC + ∠ABC is less than two right angles [1.17]. Since ∠EDF = ∠BAC, the constructed ray and the ray DF therefore meet on that side of DE [P5], producing the straight lines as necessary [P2]. Call their intersection G. Thus ∠DEG = ∠ABC, and G lies on the ray DF.

**Step 3.** Since DG and DF have the same direction, ∠EDG = ∠EDF [CN4], and hence ∠EDG = ∠BAC [CN1]. Together with ∠DEG = ∠ABC, this gives ∠DGE = ∠ACB: the angles of each triangle sum to two right angles, and subtracting the equal sums of the first two angles leaves equal third angles [1.32, CN2, CN3]. Thus triangles ABC and DEG are equiangular.

**Step 4.** Path choice: apply 6.4 directly to these equiangular triangles, obtaining AB:AC = DE:DG [6.4]. But AB:AC = DE:DF by hypothesis. Therefore DE:DG = DE:DF, since ratios equal to the same ratio are equal to one another [5.11].

**Step 5.** Since DE has the same ratio to DG and DF, the latter magnitudes are equal: DG = DF [5.9]. Both G and F lie on the same ray from D. If they were distinct, one of DG and DF would be a proper part of the other and therefore smaller, contrary to their equality [CN5]. Hence G and F coincide.

**Step 6.** Consequently, the angle equalities established for DEG give ∠ABC = ∠DEF and ∠ACB = ∠DFE [CN4, CN1]. Together with the given equality ∠BAC = ∠EDF, these show that ABC and DEF are equiangular. The equal angles at C and F are opposite the corresponding sides AB and DE; those at B and E are opposite AC and DF; and those at A and D are opposite BC and EF. Thus the angles subtended by corresponding sides are equal, as required.
