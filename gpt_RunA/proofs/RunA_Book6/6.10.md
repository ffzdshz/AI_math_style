## Proposition 6.10
**Statement:** To cut a given uncut straight line similarly to a given cut straight line.

**Step 1.** Let AB be the given uncut straight line, and let CD be divided at the points C₁, …, Cₙ₋₁, in that order; write C₀ = C and Cₙ = D. From A draw a straight line AX, not collinear with AB, and produce it until it is longer than CD (P1, P2). On AX take points P₁, …, Pₙ such that APᵢ = CCᵢ for each i, and put P₀ = A (1.3). These points occur in the same order as the given division points. Subtracting equal initial segments gives Pᵢ₋₁Pᵢ = Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ for every i; for i = 1 this is already a construction equality (CN3).

**Step 2.** Join PₙB (P1). Through each Pᵢ, for 1 ≤ i < n, draw a straight line parallel to PₙB, meeting the side AB at Qᵢ (1.31). Put Q₀ = A and Qₙ = B. These are the proposed division points of AB.

**Step 3.** Path choice: use the proportional cutting theorem directly, since the construction supplies parallels to a side of a triangle; introducing equiangular triangles is unnecessary. In triangle APₙB, the parallel PᵢQᵢ gives
APᵢ : PᵢPₙ = AQᵢ : QᵢB
for every 1 ≤ i < n (6.2).

**Step 4.** Taking these proportions jointly gives
APₙ : PᵢPₙ = AB : QᵢB
(5.18). Alternating therefore gives
PᵢPₙ : QᵢB = APₙ : AB
(5.16). The same equality holds for i = 0, since P₀ = Q₀ = A. Thus every nonzero terminal segment on APₙ has to its corresponding terminal segment on AB the same ratio APₙ : AB.

**Step 5.** Fix any i with 1 ≤ i < n. By Step 4, the two successive terminal-segment ratios are equal:
Pᵢ₋₁Pₙ : Qᵢ₋₁B = PᵢPₙ : QᵢB
(5.11). Remove the latter pair of segments from the former pair. The remainders have the same ratio as the wholes (5.19), so
Pᵢ₋₁Pᵢ : Qᵢ₋₁Qᵢ = APₙ : AB.
For i = n, this equality is already the terminal-segment equality in Step 4 with index n − 1. Consequently it holds for every corresponding pair of individual segments.

**Step 6.** For any two indices i and j, Step 5 gives
Pᵢ₋₁Pᵢ : Qᵢ₋₁Qᵢ = Pⱼ₋₁Pⱼ : Qⱼ₋₁Qⱼ
(5.11). Alternating yields
Pᵢ₋₁Pᵢ : Pⱼ₋₁Pⱼ = Qᵢ₋₁Qᵢ : Qⱼ₋₁Qⱼ
(5.16). Replacing the auxiliary segments by the equal given segments established in Step 1 gives
Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ : Cⱼ₋₁Cⱼ = Qᵢ₋₁Qᵢ : Qⱼ₋₁Qⱼ
(5.7, 5.11). Thus the segments of AB, in their corresponding order, are proportional to the segments of CD (D5.6). Therefore AB has been cut similarly to the given cut straight line CD, as required.
