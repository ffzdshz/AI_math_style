## Proposition 6.13
**Statement:** To two given straight lines to find a mean proportional.

**Step 1.** Let AB be the first given straight line. Produce AB beyond B sufficiently far, and cut off BC equal to the second given straight line, so that A, B, C lie in this order on one straight line. [P2, 1.3]

**Step 2.** Bisect AC at O, and describe the circle with centre O and radius OA. Since OA = OC, both A and C lie on the circle, and AC is a diameter. [1.10, P3, D1.15, D1.17]

**Step 3.** At B construct a perpendicular to AC, and produce it to meet the circle at D on either side of AC. Join AD and CD. The angles ABD and DBC are right angles. [1.11, P2, P1, D1.10]

**Step 4.** Since AC is a diameter, the angle ADC is an angle in a semicircle and is therefore right. Thus DB is the perpendicular from the right-angle vertex of triangle ADC to its opposite side AC. [3.31; Step 3] The direct path is now the right-triangle altitude similarity of 6.8, whose configuration has been constructed exactly.

**Step 5.** By 6.8, triangles ABD and DBC are similar both to triangle ADC and to one another. Their correspondence is (A, B, D) ↔ (D, B, C): their right-angle vertices are B, while their angles at A and C respectively coincide with the corresponding angles of the whole triangle. Hence their corresponding sides about the right angles are proportional:
AB : BD = BD : BC. [6.8, D6.1]

**Step 6.** AB is the first given straight line, and BC equals the second. Replacing BC by that equal given line leaves the ratio unchanged. Therefore the first given straight line is to BD as BD is to the second given straight line. Thus BD is the required mean proportional. [Step 1, D5.5, D5.6]
