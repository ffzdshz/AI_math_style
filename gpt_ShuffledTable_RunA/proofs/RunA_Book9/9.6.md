## Proposition 9.6
**Statement:** If a number by multiplying itself make a cube number, it will itself also be cube.

**Step 1.** Let A be the given number, and let B = A·A be cube, as hypothesized. Form C = B·A. Since C = (A·A)·A is contained by three equal numbers, C is cube. [D7.19]

**Step 2.** Apply the cube-factor criterion directly, since both the factor B and the product C are cube: B, by multiplying A, makes the cube number C. Therefore the multiplied number A is itself cube, as required. [9.5]
