## Proposition 9.4
**Statement:** If a cube number by multiplying a cube number make some number, the product will be cube.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be cube numbers, and let C be the product made by A multiplying B. Let D be the product made by A multiplying itself. Since A is cube, D is cube. [9.3]

**Step 2.** Multiplication of A and B by the same number A gives D and C respectively. Therefore D:C = A:B. [7.17]

**Step 3.** Apply the cube-ratio criterion directly, since Step 2 supplies precisely its ratio hypothesis: D and C have to one another the ratio of the cube numbers A and B, and D is cube. Hence C is cube. Thus a cube number multiplying a cube number produces a cube number. [8.25]
