## Proposition 9.24
**Statement:** If from an even number an even number be subtracted, the remainder will be even.

**Step 1.** Let B be the even number subtracted from the greater even number A. We use the definition of evenness directly: divide A into two equal parts and B into two equal parts. [D7.6]

**Step 2.** From each half of A subtract one half of B. These subtractions are possible because B is less than A, so its halves are less than the corresponding halves of A. Since equals have been subtracted from equals, the two remaining parts are equal. Together they constitute exactly the remainder after B is subtracted from A. [CN3]

**Step 3.** Thus the remainder is divisible into two equal parts and is therefore even. Hence, if an even number is subtracted from an even number, the remainder is even. [D7.6]
