## Proposition 9.9
**Statement:** If as many numbers as we please beginning from an unit be in continued proportion, and the number after the unit be square, all the rest will also be square.

**Step 1.** Let the continued proportion be 1, A₁, A₂, …, Aₙ, with A₁ square by hypothesis. If n = 1, there is nothing further to prove. Otherwise A₂, the third term counting the unit, is square by Proposition 9.8. Thus A₁ and A₂ are both square. [9.8]

**Step 2.** Choose the direct square-ratio propagation route: A₁:A₂ is now a ratio of two square numbers. By continued proportion, every successive pair Aᵢ, Aᵢ₊₁ has this same ratio. Proposition 8.24 therefore implies that whenever Aᵢ is square, Aᵢ₊₁ is also square. [8.24]

**Step 3.** Beginning with the given square A₁ and applying Step 2 successively through the finite sequence, every Aᵢ is square. Hence, if the number after the unit is square, all the remaining numbers in the continued proportion are also square. [8.24]
