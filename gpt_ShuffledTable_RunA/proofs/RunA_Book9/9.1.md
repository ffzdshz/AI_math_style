## Proposition 9.1
**Statement:** If two similar plane numbers by multiplying one another make some number, the product will be square.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be the given similar plane numbers, and let P be their product. Choose the direct mean-proportional route, since VIII.18 applies immediately to the hypothesis: there is a number M such that A:M = M:B. [8.18]

**Step 2.** Apply VII.19 to the four proportional numbers A, M, M, B. The product of the first and fourth equals the product of the second and third. Hence P = A·B = M·M. [7.19]

**Step 3.** A number produced by multiplying a number by itself is a square number. Since P = M·M, the product of the two given similar plane numbers is square. [D7.18]
