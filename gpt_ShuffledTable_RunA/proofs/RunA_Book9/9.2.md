## Proposition 9.2
**Statement:** If two numbers by multiplying one another make a square number, they are similar plane numbers.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be numbers whose product is square. By the definition of a square number, there is a number C such that AB = CC. [D7.18]

**Step 2.** Take the direct route through a mean proportional. Apply the converse of VII.19 to the four numbers A, C, C, B: since the product of the first and fourth equals that of the second and third, A:C = C:B. Thus C is a mean proportional between A and B. [7.19]

**Step 3.** Numbers between which there is one mean proportional are similar plane numbers. Therefore A and B are similar plane numbers, as required. [8.20]
