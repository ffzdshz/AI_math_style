## Proposition 9.28
**Statement:** If an odd number by multiplying an even number make some number, the product will be even.

**Step 1.** Let the odd number A, by multiplying the even number B, produce C. By the definition of multiplication, C is the sum of as many copies of B as there are units in A. We use this direct repeated-addition route because the number being repeated is already even. [D7.15]

**Step 2.** Each of these copies of B is even. Since the sum of any multitude of even numbers is even, C is even. Therefore, if an odd number multiplies an even number, the product is even. [9.21]
