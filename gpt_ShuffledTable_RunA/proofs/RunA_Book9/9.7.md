## Proposition 9.7
**Statement:** If a composite number by multiplying any number make some number, the product will be solid.

**Step 1.** Let the composite number A, by multiplying any number B, produce C. We use the definitions directly, since it suffices to exhibit three numerical factors of C [D7.17]. Since A is composite, choose a proper numerical measure D of A, and let E be the number of times D measures A [D7.13].

**Step 2.** By the definition of multiplication, E multiplying D produces A. Therefore, multiplying E and D, and then multiplying their product A by B, produces C [D7.15 and the hypothesis].

**Step 3.** Thus the three numbers E, D, and B, by successive multiplication, produce C. By the definition of a solid number, C is solid, as required [D7.17].
