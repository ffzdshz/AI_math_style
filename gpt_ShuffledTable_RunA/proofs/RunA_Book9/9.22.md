## Proposition 9.22
**Statement:** If as many odd numbers as we please be added together, and their multitude be even, the whole will be even.

**Step 1.** Let an arbitrary multitude of odd numbers be given, and suppose their multitude is even. Choose the direct route of removing one unit from each odd number. Each remainder is even, by the definition of an odd number. [D7.7]

**Step 2.** Add all these even remainders together, and call their sum R. The sum R is even, since any multitude of even numbers has an even sum. [9.21]

**Step 3.** Call the sum of the removed units U. There is exactly one removed unit for each given odd number, so U is the number expressing their multitude. Since that multitude is even by hypothesis, U is even. [D7.2]

**Step 4.** The whole sum of the given odd numbers is R together with U. Both are even, so their sum is even. Therefore, if any even multitude of odd numbers is added together, the whole is even. [9.21]
