## Proposition 5.21
**Statement:** If there be three magnitudes, and others equal to them in multitude, which taken two and two together are in the same ratio, and the proportion of them be perturbed, then, if ex aequali the first magnitude is greater than the third, the fourth will also be greater than the sixth; if equal, equal; and if less, less.

**Step 1.** Let the two triples be A, B, C and D, E, F. The perturbed proportions are
A:B = E:F and B:C = D:E (D5.18).
We take the direct comparison route: invert the second proportion to obtain ratios with the common antecedent E, then use the earlier results comparing magnitudes through their ratios.

**Step 2.** From B:C = D:E, obtain C:B = E:D directly by D5.5. Indeed, for arbitrary positive integers m and n, the comparison of nB with mC agrees with that of nD with mE. Reversing both comparisons, the comparison of mC with nB agrees with that of mE with nD. This is precisely C:B = E:D (D5.5).

**Step 3.** Suppose A > C. Then A:B > C:B (5.8). By D5.7, there are positive integers m and n such that
mA > nB, while mC ≤ nB.
Since A:B = E:F, the first comparison gives mE > nF; since C:B = E:D, the second gives mE ≤ nD (D5.5). Thus E:F > E:D by D5.7. Of magnitudes to which the same magnitude has ratios, that to which it has the greater ratio is less (5.10). Hence F < D, or D > F.

**Step 4.** Suppose A = C. Equal magnitudes have the same ratio to the same magnitude, so A:B = C:B (5.7). Combining this with A:B = E:F and C:B = E:D gives E:F = E:D (5.11). Magnitudes to which the same magnitude has the same ratio are equal (5.9); hence F = D.

**Step 5.** Suppose A < C. Then C:B > A:B (5.8). By D5.7, there are positive integers m and n such that
mC > nB, while mA ≤ nB.
The proportions C:B = E:D and A:B = E:F therefore give mE > nD and mE ≤ nF respectively (D5.5). Thus E:D > E:F (D5.7), whence D < F (5.10).

Consequently, if the first magnitude A is greater than, equal to, or less than the third C, the fourth D is respectively greater than, equal to, or less than the sixth F, as required.
