## Proposition 5.5
**Statement:** If a magnitude be the same multiple of a magnitude that a part subtracted is of a part subtracted, the remainder will also be the same multiple of the remainder that the whole is of the whole.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be the whole magnitudes, and let C and D be the respective parts subtracted. Suppose A is the same multiple of B that C is of D. Choose the direct application of resource 5.1: its subtraction rule has exactly these hypotheses, and it is order-valid because it is in Book V with 1 < 5. [5.1]

**Step 2.** Apply 5.1 to the wholes A, B and their respective subtracted parts C, D. It follows that the remainder A − C is the same multiple of the remainder B − D that the whole A is of the whole B. This is the required conclusion. [5.1]
