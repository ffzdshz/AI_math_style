## Proposition 5.24
**Statement:** If a first magnitude have to a second the same ratio as a third has to a fourth, and also a fifth have to the second the same ratio as a sixth to the fourth, the first and fifth added together will have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth have to the fourth.

**Step 1.** Let A, B, C, D, E, F be the first through sixth magnitudes. By hypothesis, A:B = C:D and E:B = F:D. The latter equality gives B:E = D:F directly by the definition of equal ratios: for arbitrary positive integers m,n, the comparisons of nE with mB and of nF with mD agree, so their reversed comparisons also agree. [D5.5]

**Step 2.** Apply ex aequali to the triples A, B, E and C, D, F. Since A:B = C:D and B:E = D:F, it follows that A:E = C:F. The common intermediate terms B and D make ordinary ex aequali the direct choice here. [5.22]

**Step 3.** Compose the proportion A:E = C:F. Composition takes each antecedent together with its consequent in relation to that consequent; hence (A+E):E = (C+F):F. [D5.14, 5.18]

**Step 4.** Apply ex aequali to the triples A+E, E, B and C+F, F, D, using Step 3 and the given equality E:B = F:D. We obtain (A+E):B = (C+F):D. Thus the first and fifth magnitudes added together have to the second the same ratio as the third and sixth added together have to the fourth, as required. [5.22]
