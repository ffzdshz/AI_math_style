## Proposition 7.26
**Statement:** If two numbers be prime to two numbers, both to each, their products also will be prime to one another.

**Step 1.** Let A and B each be prime to each of C and D, and write P = AB and Q = CD. Being prime to one another is symmetric, since it means that the unit alone is a common measure (D7.12).

**Step 2.** Choose the direct product rule, 7.24, because the hypotheses give two numbers prime to each fixed factor. Since A and B are both prime to C, their product P is prime to C. Likewise, since A and B are both prime to D, P is prime to D (7.24).

**Step 3.** Thus C and D are both prime to P, by symmetry (D7.12). Applying 7.24 to C and D with P fixed, their product Q is prime to P. Therefore AB and CD are prime to one another, as required.
