## Proposition 7.32
**Statement:** Any number either is prime or is measured by some prime number.

**Step 1.** Let N be any number. If N is prime, the first alternative holds. Otherwise, by the definition of a prime number, N has a measure other than the unit; hence N is composite by the definition of a composite number. (D7.11, D7.13)

**Step 2.** In the composite case, apply VII.31 directly, since it supplies exactly the required prime measure: some prime number measures N. Thus, in either case, N either is prime or is measured by some prime number. (7.31)
