## Proposition 7.4
**Statement:** Any number is either a part or parts of any number, the less of the greater.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be any numbers with A less than B. Take the direct definitional route: the definitions of “part” and “parts” distinguish whether A measures B or does not measure B, and these cases are exhaustive. [D7.3, D7.4]

**Step 2.** If A measures B, then A is a part of B, by the definition of a number being a part of a greater number. [D7.3]

**Step 3.** If A does not measure B, then A is parts of B, by the definition of parts. Hence in either case the less number is either a part or parts of the greater, as required. [D7.4]
