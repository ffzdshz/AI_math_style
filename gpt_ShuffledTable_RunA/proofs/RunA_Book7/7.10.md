## Proposition 7.10
**Statement:** If a number be parts of a number, and another be the same parts of another, alternately also, whatever parts or part the first is of the third, the same parts or the same part will the second also be of the fourth.

**Step 1.** Let A be parts of B, and let C be the same parts of D. Consider the asserted part-or-parts comparison of A with C, so that A<C. Unpack the hypothesis of “the same parts”: there are constituent measures e and f and counts r,s, with 1<r<s, such that
A=r·e, B=s·e, C=r·f, D=s·f.
Here each product denotes repeated addition: A and C contain equally many corresponding parts, and those parts measure B and D equally many times. Since A<C, necessarily e<f. [D7.2, D7.3, D7.4]

**Step 2.** First dispose of the possibility that e is a unit. Let f contain t units. Arrange the units of C in r rows, each containing t units. Its t columns then each contain r units and hence each equal A. Likewise, arranging D in s rows of t units gives t columns, each equal to B. Thus C consists of t copies of A, and D of t copies of B. Consequently A is the same part of C that B is of D, proving the conclusion in this case. [D7.2, CN2, D7.3]

**Step 3.** Now let e be a number; then f, being greater, is also a number. By VII.4, e is either a part or parts of f. Moreover, e is the same part of A that f is of C, since each measures its respective whole r times. Similarly, e is the same part of B that f is of D, since each measures its respective whole s times. We therefore take the direct single-part route through VII.9. [7.4, D7.3]

**Step 4.** Apply VII.9 to e,A,f,C. It follows that A is the same part or the same parts of C that e is of f. Apply VII.9 again, now to e,B,f,D. It follows that B is the same part or the same parts of D that e is of f. [7.9]

**Step 5.** Both comparisons therefore give the same part or the same collection of parts, since each agrees with the comparison of e with f. Hence, alternately, whatever parts or part the first number A is of the third C, the same parts or the same part the second B is of the fourth D. This is the required conclusion. [CN1]
