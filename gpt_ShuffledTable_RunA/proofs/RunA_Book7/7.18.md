## Proposition 7.18
**Statement:** If two numbers by multiplying any number make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will have the same ratio as the multipliers.

**Step 1.** Let the numbers A and B, by multiplying a number C, produce D and E respectively. We choose to reverse the multiplications, since this gives a common multiplier directly. By 7.16, C multiplying A produces D, and C multiplying B produces E. [7.16]

**Step 2.** Since the same number C multiplies A and B to produce D and E, proposition 7.17 gives D:E = A:B. Thus the numbers produced have the same ratio as the original multipliers, as required. [7.17]
