## Proposition 7.16
**Statement:** If two numbers by multiplying one another make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will be equal to one another.

**Step 1.** Let A by multiplying B produce C, and let B by multiplying A produce D. Let u be a unit. By the definition of multiplication, B measures C as many times as u measures A; likewise, A measures D as many times as u measures B. [D7.15]

**Step 2.** Use the direct route of alternating these equal multiplicities. Since u measures A as many times as B measures C, Proposition 7.15 gives that u measures B as many times as A measures C. [7.15]

**Step 3.** Thus A measures both C and D as many times as there are units in B. Consequently, C and D are each equal to the sum obtained by adding A to itself that many times. Things equal to the same thing are equal to one another, so C = D. Therefore the numbers produced by the two multiplications are equal. [D7.15, CN1]
