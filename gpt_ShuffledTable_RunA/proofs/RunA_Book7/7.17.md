## Proposition 7.17
**Statement:** If a number by multiplying two numbers make certain numbers, the numbers so produced will have the same ratio as the numbers multiplied.

**Step 1.** Let the number A, by multiplying the numbers B and C, produce D and E respectively. By the definition of multiplication, D consists of as many copies of B as there are units in A, and E consists of that same number of copies of C. Thus D is the same multiple of B that E is of C. [D7.15, D7.5]

**Step 2.** By the same-multiple clause in the definition of proportional numbers, D:B = E:C. This proportion follows directly from the given multiplication, so the direct route is to alternate it. [D7.20]

**Step 3.** Four proportional numbers are also proportional alternately. Applying this to D, B, E, C gives D:E = B:C. Therefore the numbers produced have the same ratio as the numbers multiplied, as required. [7.13]
