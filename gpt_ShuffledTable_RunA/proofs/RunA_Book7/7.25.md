## Proposition 7.25
**Statement:** If two numbers be prime to one another, the product of one of them into itself will be prime to the remaining one.

**Step 1.** Let A and B be numbers prime to one another. Both factors in the product A·A are therefore prime to B, by the hypothesis.

**Step 2.** Choose the direct product rule VII.24: numbers prime to the same number have their product prime to that number. Apply it with both factors equal to A and the common number equal to B; the rule does not require distinct factors. Hence A·A is prime to B, which is the required conclusion. [7.24]
