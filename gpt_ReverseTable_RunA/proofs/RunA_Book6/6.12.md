## Proposition 6.12
**Statement:** To three given straight lines to find a fourth proportional.

**Step 1.** Let the three given straight lines have lengths a, b, and c, in that order. Draw two non-collinear rays from O. Produce their straight lines as necessary and cut off OA = a and AC = b consecutively on the first ray, so that O, A, C occur in that order. On the second ray cut off OB = c. [P1, P2, 1.3]

**Step 2.** Join A to B. Through C draw a straight line parallel to AB, meeting the second ray produced beyond B at D. Thus AB is parallel to CD in triangle OCD. [P1, P2, 1.31]

**Step 3.** Use the forward direction of the proportional-section theorem directly, since the construction supplies a parallel to a side of a triangle. Applied to triangle OCD, it gives OA : AC = OB : BD. Substituting the lengths prescribed in Step 1 yields a : b = c : BD. Therefore BD is a fourth proportional to the three given straight lines, as required. [6.2]
