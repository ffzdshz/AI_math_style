## Proposition 6.10
**Statement:** To cut a given uncut straight line similarly to a given cut straight line.

**Step 1.** Let AB be the given uncut straight line, and suppose the given cut straight line has n successive parts, where n ≥ 2. Draw a ray from A not collinear with AB. Extending it as necessary, copy the given parts successively onto it, obtaining points A = C₀, C₁, …, Cₙ, with each Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ equal to the corresponding given part. These transfers and markings are possible by I.2 and I.3. [P1, P2, 1.2, 1.3]

**Step 2.** Join CₙB. Through each interior point Cᵢ draw a parallel to CₙB, meeting AB at Bᵢ. Put B₀ = A and Bₙ = B. The points Bᵢ occur successively along AB, and all the lines CᵢBᵢ, for 1 ≤ i ≤ n, are parallel to one another. Our direct route is to apply the parallel-cutting theorem VI.2 to this construction. [P1, 1.31, 1.30]

**Step 3.** In triangle AC₂B₂, the line C₁B₁ is parallel to C₂B₂. Therefore
C₀C₁ : C₁C₂ = B₀B₁ : B₁B₂.
Thus the first two parts have the required ratio. [6.2]

**Step 4.** Consider any index i with 2 ≤ i ≤ n − 1. Through Cᵢ₋₁ draw a straight line parallel to AB, meeting CᵢBᵢ at Q and Cᵢ₊₁Bᵢ₊₁ at R. The points Cᵢ₋₁, Q, R occur in that order. [1.31]

**Step 5.** The quadrilaterals Bᵢ₋₁BᵢQCᵢ₋₁ and BᵢBᵢ₊₁RQ are parallelograms: one pair of their opposite sides is parallel to AB, and the other pair lies on the mutually parallel lines constructed in Step 2. Hence their opposite sides are equal:
Cᵢ₋₁Q = Bᵢ₋₁Bᵢ, and QR = BᵢBᵢ₊₁. [1.30, 1.34]

**Step 6.** In triangle Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ₊₁R, the line CᵢQ is parallel to Cᵢ₊₁R. Consequently,
Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ : CᵢCᵢ₊₁ = Cᵢ₋₁Q : QR.
Substituting the equal lengths from Step 5 gives
Cᵢ₋₁Cᵢ : CᵢCᵢ₊₁ = Bᵢ₋₁Bᵢ : BᵢBᵢ₊₁.
This holds for every index considered in Step 4. [6.2, D5.5]

**Step 7.** Together, Steps 3 and 6 show that every two successive parts of AB have the same ratio as the corresponding successive parts on ACₙ. Those parts were copied equal to the parts of the given cut straight line, so their ratios are unchanged. Therefore the points B₁, …, Bₙ₋₁ cut the given straight line AB similarly to the given cut straight line, as required. [D5.5]
