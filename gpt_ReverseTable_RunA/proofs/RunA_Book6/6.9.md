## Proposition 6.9
**Statement:** From a given straight line to cut off a prescribed part.

**Step 1.** Let AB be the given straight line, and let the prescribed part be its nth part, where n ≥ 2 is a prescribed integer. We must cut off a segment which measures AB exactly n times. [D5.1, D5.2]

**Step 2.** Draw an auxiliary ray from A, not along AB, and choose a segment AD on it. Produce the ray and successively cut off n − 1 further segments, each equal to AD; call the final endpoint C. Thus D lies between A and C, and DC is the sum of n − 1 segments equal to AD, so DC = (n − 1)AD. [P1, P2, 1.3, CN2, D5.2]

**Step 3.** Join CB. Through D draw a straight line parallel to CB, meeting AB at F. [P1, 1.31]

**Step 4.** In triangle ACB, the parallel DF cuts its sides proportionally. Hence
CD : DA = BF : FA.
We use this proportion between the pieces directly, without converting it into a proportion between whole sides. [6.2]

**Step 5.** Apply the definition of equal ratios to this proportion: take one copy of each antecedent CD and BF, and n − 1 copies of each consequent DA and FA. Since CD equals (n − 1)DA, the corresponding comparison must also be equality. Therefore BF = (n − 1)FA. [D5.5]

**Step 6.** Adding AF to these equal magnitudes gives
AB = AF + FB = AF + (n − 1)AF = nAF.
Thus AF measures AB exactly n times, and is the prescribed nth part of AB. The required part has therefore been cut off. [CN2, D5.1, D5.2]
