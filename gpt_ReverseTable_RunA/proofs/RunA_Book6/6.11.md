## Proposition 6.11
**Statement:** To two given straight lines to find a third proportional.

**Step 1.** Let the two given straight lines have lengths a and b. Draw two rays from A forming an angle. On these rays cut off AB = a and AC = b, producing the rays as necessary. [P1, P2, 1.3]

**Step 2.** Produce AB beyond B to D, making BD = b. Join BC, and through D draw DE parallel to BC, meeting AC produced beyond C at E. [P1, P2, 1.3, 1.31]

**Step 3.** In triangle ADE, the straight line BC is parallel to DE and cuts the sides AD and AE. Therefore
AB : BD = AC : CE.
This parallel-section route gives the required proportion directly, without passing through similar triangles. [6.2]

**Step 4.** Since AB = a and BD = AC = b, the proportion becomes
a : b = b : CE.
Thus CE is a third proportional to the two given straight lines, as required. [D5.6]
