## Proposition 3.35
**Statement:** If in a circle two straight lines cut one another, the rectangle contained by the segments of the one is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments of the other.

**Step 1.** Let the chords AB and CD intersect inside the circle at E. Write R(u,v) for the rectangle contained by the straight lines u,v, and S(u) for the square on u. We use the midpoint-and-square route: II.5 applies directly to the internal division of each chord, while III.3 supplies the required right angles. [D2.1, D1.22, 2.5, 3.3]

**Step 2.** Find the centre O, and join O to A, B, C, D, and, if distinct from O, to E. The radii OA, OB, OC, OD are equal. If E=O, the four segments EA, EB, EC, ED are therefore equal; each of the two rectangles in question is the square on this same length, so they are equal. Henceforth suppose E≠O. [3.1, P1, D1.15, D2.1, D1.22, CN4, CN1]

**Step 3.** Apply the following argument to each chord in turn, writing its endpoints as X,Y. Bisect XY at H. We shall establish
R(XE,EY)+S(OE)=S(OX).
First suppose that XY passes through O. Since OX=OY, its midpoint H is O. The chord is thus bisected at O and divided unequally at E. By II.5,
R(XE,EY)+S(OE)=S(OX),
as required. [1.10, D1.15, 2.5]

**Step 4.** Now suppose that XY does not pass through O. Join OH. Since the line through O bisects XY, it is perpendicular to XY. If H=E, then XE=EY, and the rectangle R(XE,EY) is S(EX). The right-angled triangle OEX therefore gives
R(XE,EY)+S(OE)=S(EX)+S(OE)=S(OX).
This establishes the required equality in this case. [P1, 3.3, D2.1, D1.22, CN4, 1.47]

**Step 5.** It remains, for a chord not through O, to consider H≠E. The triangles OHX and OHE are right-angled at H, so
S(OX)=S(OH)+S(HX),
S(OE)=S(OH)+S(HE).
Since XY is bisected at H and divided unequally at E, II.5 gives
R(XE,EY)+S(HE)=S(HX).
Adding S(OH) to both sides and substituting the two right-triangle equalities yields
R(XE,EY)+S(OE)=S(OX).
Thus the required equality holds for either chord in every case. [3.3, 1.47, 2.5, CN2, CN1]

**Step 6.** Applying Steps 3–5 first to AB and then to CD gives
R(AE,EB)+S(OE)=S(OA),
R(CE,ED)+S(OE)=S(OC).
Since OA=OC, their squares are equal. Consequently,
R(AE,EB)+S(OE)=R(CE,ED)+S(OE). [D1.15, CN4, CN1]

**Step 7.** Subtract the common square S(OE). The remainders are equal:
R(AE,EB)=R(CE,ED).
Therefore the rectangle contained by the segments of one chord is equal to the rectangle contained by the segments of the other. [CN3]
