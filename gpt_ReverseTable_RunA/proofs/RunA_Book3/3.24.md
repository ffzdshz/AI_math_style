## Proposition 3.24
**Statement:** Similar segments of circles on equal straight lines are equal to one another.

**Step 1.** Let S and T be similar segments of circles on equal straight lines AB and DE. By the definition of similar segments, the angles admitted by S and T are equal. [D3.11]

**Step 2.** Apply S to the position of T, placing A on D and AB along DE, with the two segments on the same side of DE. Since AB equals DE, B falls on E. Denote the placed segment by S′. This application preserves the figure and its angles: corresponding figures and angles coincide under superposition and therefore are equal. Thus S′ equals S and remains similar to T. [CN4, D3.11]

**Step 3.** Use III.23 directly, since the application has put the segments on the same straight line and on the same side. That proposition forbids two similar and unequal segments in this position; hence S′ and T are equal. Since S also equals S′, S and T are equal. Therefore similar segments of circles on equal straight lines are equal to one another. [3.23, CN1]
