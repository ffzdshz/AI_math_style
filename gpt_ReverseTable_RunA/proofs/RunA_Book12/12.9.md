## Proposition 12.9
**Statement:** In equal pyramids which have triangular bases the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights; and those pyramids in which the bases are reciprocally proportional to the heights are equal.

**Step 1.** Let the two triangular pyramids be P and Q, with base areas b and c and respective heights h and k. We must prove that P = Q if and only if b:c = k:h. We use completion to prisms and parallelepipeds, since the reciprocal-base-and-height criterion is already established for parallelepipeds in [11.34].

**Step 2.** By drawing parallels, complete each pyramid to a triangular prism, taking the pyramid’s apex as a vertex of the opposite triangular face. Complete each base triangle to a parallelogram, and correspondingly complete each prism to a parallelepiped. These constructions use [1.31]; the opposite base planes are parallel by [11.15]. Thus the completed parallelepipeds, denoted U and V, still have heights h and k. Since a diagonal bisects a parallelogram, their base areas are respectively 2b and 2c [1.34].

**Step 3.** Apply the division of a triangular prism into three equal pyramids, with the original pyramid as the piece having the prism’s entire lower triangular base. Each prism is therefore three times its original pyramid [12.7]. The plane through the corresponding diagonals of the opposite parallelogram faces bisects the completed parallelepiped, making the triangular prism one of its halves [11.28]. Consequently,
U = 6P and V = 6Q,
where these multiples mean sums of equal copies.

**Step 4.** Parts have the same ratio as their corresponding equal multiples. Hence the ratio of the completed bases is
2b:2c = b:c [5.15].

**Step 5.** Suppose first that P = Q. Adding equal copies gives U = V [CN2]. The bases of equal parallelepipeds are reciprocally proportional to their heights, so
2b:2c = k:h [11.34].
Combining this with Step 4 gives b:c = k:h [5.11]. Thus equal triangular pyramids have bases reciprocally proportional to their heights.

**Step 6.** Conversely, suppose b:c = k:h. Step 4 and [5.11] give 2b:2c = k:h. Therefore the completed parallelepipeds are equal: U = V [11.34]. By [5.15],
P:Q = U:V and Q:Q = V:V.
Since U = V, we have U:V = V:V [5.7]. These ratio equalities give P:Q = Q:Q [5.11], and hence P = Q [5.9]. Thus triangular pyramids whose bases are reciprocally proportional to their heights are equal, completing both directions.
